A replay of 41,176 real bank telemarketing leads. Leads are ranked by a model score, and any lead that needed more attempts than your cap is counted as a lost sale, so the estimate is conservative. Move the sliders to test a different policy.

Data

UCI Bank Marketing, 41,188 calls from a Portuguese bank campaign (Moro, Cortez and Rita, 2014; CC BY 4.0). 12 duplicates removed.

Model

Logistic regression, AUC 0.80 on a hold-out set. Scores here are 5-fold cross-validated, so no lead is scored by a model that saw it. Call duration is excluded because it is only known after the call.

Limits

This is a historical replay, not a live trial. A real rollout should be tested against a control group before any budget is moved.
